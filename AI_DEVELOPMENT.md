# AI development disclosure

Foundry: Bulk Commerce was developed with substantial assistance from OpenAI ChatGPT/Codex. AI assistance included compatibility research and API inspection, architecture proposals, implementation code, automated regression tests, build/debugging work and documentation. This was more than autocomplete or locating method names.

The Panda Foundry’s project owner defined requirements and constraints, acted as project architect and acceptance tester, directed revisions and performed live Minecraft acceptance testing. Automated results and owner-reported gameplay results are identified separately in the documentation. Release review, publication decisions and operational responsibility remain with The Panda Foundry.

AI-generated work can contain errors. Neither AI assistance nor passing tests establishes security, legal clearance or guaranteed cross-resource durability. Third-party implementation references were inspected for interoperability; their licences and attribution remain applicable. No claim is made that the owner hand-wrote all implementation code.
